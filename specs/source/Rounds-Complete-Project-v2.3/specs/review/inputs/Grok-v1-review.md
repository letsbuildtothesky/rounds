## Owner decisions still required (do not invent)

E10’s list is real, not ceremony:

- Cancellation / wait / return tariffs (else P15 forbids automatic money)
- Retention seconds per class (else E08 gates production data)
- Role bundles vs city\_grants.capabilities[] (two sources of authority)
- Slot hold TTL, proof matrix defaults, logo square vs Dispatch dot
- Routing + voice + KYC + channel providers
- Whether freelance compensation is recorded-only or Rounds initiates payout (P15 says not a wallet)

Until those are accepted, corresponding production switches stay off — the specs already say that; do not fill numbers in build specs.

## What is in good shape

- Slot admission vs vehicle cargo/stops vs return/reload are actually separated (P03/P04), including overcommit-without-deleting-bookings.
- Freelance accept lock order + UNIQUE (broadcast) + global exclusion is the right concurrency story.
- Handoff ≠ proof durable; late offline proof becomes an incident, not a merge.
- Broadcast not auto-started when own fleet is short — consistent across P01/P03/P07/P13.
- Tracking token is its own security scheme; surprise privacy is specified.
- No cascade deletes; same-tenant composite FKs exist for most tenant objects.
- Deferred Lalamove and temperature are consistently out of the active graph.
